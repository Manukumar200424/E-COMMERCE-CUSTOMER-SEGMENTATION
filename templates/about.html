Project reference

Turning customer behavior into a clearer next move.

A beginner-friendly academic project demonstrating the full path from a CSV dataset to an interactive machine learning dashboard.

01 / The idea

What is customer segmentation?

Customer segmentation divides a broad customer base into smaller groups whose members share meaningful characteristics. In e-commerce, this helps teams move beyond one-size-fits-all messaging and prioritize the customers most likely to respond.

02 / The method

Why K-Means?

K-Means is an unsupervised algorithm. It learns four cluster centers from standardized income, purchase, spending, order value, frequency, visit, and cart behavior, then assigns each customer to its nearest center.
